How the stylist works

The stylist uses the information you provide and matches it with the C-LHOUETTE collection. It can consider:

  • Occasion
  • Size
  • Budget
  • Style preference
  • Skin-tone preference
  • Keywords such as elegant, trendy, romantic, classic, minimalist, satin, etc.
Tip: You can add more dress information in the INVENTORY section of this file, including colors and skin-tone-friendly colors. The stylist will then use those details in its recommendations.
